How it works

STEP 01

Watch the market

Stream microsecond-sequenced Binance depth updates and live public trades to reconstruct a synchronized Level 2 order book with zero data loss.

STEP 02

Post a bid and ask

Calculate reservation fair value, apply inventory skew, and continuously place optimal two-sided limit quotes around the prevailing midpoint.

// ALGORITHMS //

The three strategies

Fixed spread

Places symmetric buy and sell limit orders at a fixed dollar spread around the current order book midpoint.

Avellaneda-Stoikov

Dynamically shifts bid and ask quotes based on current inventory deviation and asset volatility to drive inventory back to target.

Avellaneda-Stoikov with order-book imbalance

Enhances the inventory model by factoring in immediate depth imbalance between top bid and ask volumes to predict short-term price drift.
